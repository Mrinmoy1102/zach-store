Our story

It started with a folding table.

Photo to come: the original folding table

Zach Store started as a folding table in the corner of a repair shop, with six phones, two laptops and a cardboard box of cables. Zach had one rule, written on an index card taped to the table:

Nobody should leave feeling they overpaid.

Customers noticed. A woman named Dana came in on a Tuesday needing a phone for her father, who had dropped his third one that year. She had $200, and she said so.

The rest of the story is still being written up.
