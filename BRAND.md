# Zach Store brand sheet

Confirmed by the owner on 2026-10-23. Every value below lives as a CSS variable in `styles.css` (`:root`). Components use the variables, never raw values.

## Colour

| Name | Hex | Variable | Role |
|---|---|---|---|
| Night Counter | `#0E0D0C` | `--c-night` | Page background |
| Shelf | `#181614` | `--c-shelf` | Raised surfaces: labels, drawer, plates |
| Bone | `#F1ECE4` | `--c-bone` | Main text on dark; ground of the "For teams" chapter |
| Pencil | `#A79F94` | `--c-pencil` | Secondary text on dark |
| Sun Red | `#F04A2E` | `--c-sun` | The logo's sun. Actions and the budget ruler only |
| Brush Ink | `#171412` | `--c-ink` | Text on Bone |

Derived stops (same hues, for contrast on the Bone ground): Sun Red Deep `#B42B14`, Ink Soft `#574F46`.

## Type

- **Archivo** (variable width): headings set wide (`wdth` 112), body at normal width. It was already the shop's font.
- **Geist Mono**: prices on drawn data only, specs, label numbers. Never for prose.

## Voice

A straight-talking shopkeeper who tells you the full price before you ask. No hype words, no invented numbers.

## Signature

The red sun from the logo, and the budget ruler that tells you what you'd keep. Boldness is spent there; everything else stays quiet.
